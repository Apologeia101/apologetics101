Credible Faith

Credible Faith exists to help Christians be ready to give a reasoned defence for the hope within them. We train students to think critically and biblically about mainstream and controversial issues, identify faulty reasoning, and respond to challenges with clarity, humility, and kindness—even when others disagree.

Inspired by 1 Peter 3:15.
